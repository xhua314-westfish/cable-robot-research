## 1. Purpose and Research Decision

> Select a **practically buildable, adjustable, six-cable Prototype Configuration V1.0** quickly, test whether it remains useful after replacing the rigid sphere with a simplified flexible balloon, and prepare the inputs for cable-length, tension, drum, motor and sensor sizing.

**Do not require a proven global optimum to begin prototype design.** Do not describe a configuration as physically feasible merely because its idealised cable geometry passes. The six-cable system must support passive, unknown motion with low-tension cable tracking; it is **not** intended to prescribe a balloon trajectory.

### What is already completed

- B1: 280 × 300 × 320 mm engineering boundary; 550 mL rigid spherical balloon (radius approximately 50.83 mm); 26 original anchor candidates; 40 original attachment candidates.
- B2: six-cable Experiments A and B generated by reference-pose random search.
- B3: 189 sampled poses, consisting of 27 positions and 7 single-axis orientation cases. Geometric pass: A 40/189; B 105/189; B has 81 Cable 5–6 contact cases.
- B4.1: analytical/numerical Jacobian verification, scaled singular values, condition numbers and **model-predicted** pose covariance. With assumed independent cable-length noise SD 0.1 mm: median position uncertainty A 0.3860 mm, B 0.1657 mm, each evaluated on its own feasible subset. Lesson 5 final integrated output should be verified in MATLAB and archived before it is marked fully complete.
- B4.2: nonlinear forward kinematics and Monte Carlo for the **current six-cable geometry** remain to be completed; reserve them as parallel validation, not a blocker for initial mechanical-frame concepts.

### Two-week success definition

1. A **provisional**, mechanically reviewable six-cable V1.0 geometry is selected **or** an explicit blocker and the best shortlist are recorded.
2. The selected geometry includes six world-frame anchor coordinates, six body/material-frame attachment coordinates, **cable pairing**, nominal lengths and sampled length ranges.
3. Clearance, geometric coverage and Jacobian uncertainty are evaluated on a **fixed common pose set**, with independent spot-check poses; key failures are stated rather than hidden.
4. A first flexible-balloon model tests attachment migration, cable routing and rigid-versus-deformation ambiguity.
5. Hardware compatibility and adjustability are documented; B6 initial assumptions and a handoff table are ready. **Detailed B6 sizing and procurement are outside this two-week plan.**

### Research guardrails

- Never infer continuous workspace coverage from the 189 discrete samples.
- Distinguish **zero-thickness segment contact**, **finite-cable clearance**, **positive-tension equilibrium**, **sensing rank**, and **nonlinear estimation accuracy**.
- The engineering gastric box is a simplified test envelope, not a clinically measured gastric lumen.
- Never treat assumed 0.1 mm measurement noise as calibrated sensor accuracy.
- A rigid Jacobian of rank 6 does not establish simultaneous identifiability of rigid pose and extra deformation states from six scalar cable-length measurements.
- Keep **existing A/B datasets immutable** as comparison references. Record random seeds, coordinates and code versions.
- Do not start 7/8-cable configuration optimisation in these two weeks; reserve expandable mechanical and sensor interfaces.

## 3. Week 1 — Configuration Diversity and Screening (4–10 November)

### Day 1 — Wed 4 Nov | B5.1 Configuration Audit and V1.0 Requirements | **Half-day, 2–2.5 h**

**Research question:** What is fixed, what is adjustable, and why do A/B not yet provide a buildable reference?

**Tasks**

- [ ] **35 min:** Load `B1_geometry_baseline.mat`, `B2_6cable_experiment_A.mat`, `B2_experiment_B_results.mat`, B3.2 and B4.1 results. Verify variable dimensions and that each cable row defines `(A_i,r_i)` pairing.
- [ ] **35 min:** Export a six-row A/B table: anchor coordinate, anchor face, attachment coordinate, nominal length; identify anchor and attachment separations. Record B's Cable 5–6 contact issue.
- [ ] **35 min:** Define **core prototype test poses** and a wider target pose set. Keep the existing 189 poses as the common baseline; do not select only B's previously feasible poses.
- [ ] **25–45 min:** Draft an explicit V1.0 decision sheet: geometric pass, clearance placeholder, rank 6, `sigma_min`, covariance, accessibility, adjustable mounts, and unresolved tension/low-disturbance questions.

**Theory:** `B_i = P + R r_i`, `L_i = ||A_i - B_i||`; `H_s` and `rank(H_s)=6` for local rigid-pose identifiability. **Evidence:** B1–B4.1 own verified calculations and [R1], [R2].

**Output:** `B5_1_configuration_audit.md`, `B5_1_baseline_coordinates.csv`, `B5_V1_acceptance_criteria.md`.

**Acceptance:** All six A/B anchor–attachment pairs are recorded. The provisional V1.0 core pose region is **declared**, not silently chosen after scoring.

### Day 2 — Thu 5 Nov | B5.1 Candidate Region Expansion | 4–5 h

**Research question:** Does the original 26/40-point discretisation exclude useful designs?

- [ ] **45 min:** Recreate the original 26 anchor and 40 attachment candidates as an immutable baseline.
- [ ] **75 min:** Expand surface-anchor sampling to a 5×5 grid on the six box faces (**98 unique candidate points** for the regular full-surface grid); verify boundary containment, deduplication and physical access assumptions.
- [ ] **75 min:** Add approximately 80–120 quasi-uniform sphere candidates using the Fibonacci sphere as a **sampling heuristic**; maintain original 40 points and check radius tolerance, minimum attachment separation and potential mounting patches.
- [ ] **40 min:** Visualise candidate locations and mark regions to exclude (mechanical mounting, access, balloon connection strength); do not fabricate exclusion dimensions.
- [ ] **25 min:** Save generation seeds/settings, datasets and short notes.

**Theory:** spherical coordinates and surface-geometry constraints `||r_i|| = r_b`; [R1] supports workspace-constrained attachment selection. **Note:** 5×5 and Fibonacci sampling are engineering choices, not numbers endorsed by [R1].

**Output:** `B5_1_candidate_expansion.m`, `B5_1_candidate_sets.mat`, `B5_1_candidate_regions.png`.

**Acceptance:** Valid, reproducible coordinates; both candidate sets retain broad geometric diversity.

### Day 3 — Fri 6 Nov | B5.1 Configuration Families and Pairing | 4–5 h

**Research question:** How can new six-cable layouts be explored without merely perturbing Experiment B?

- [ ] **45 min:** Define three anchor-distribution families: distributed across faces, opposite-face/layered, and asymmetrical. Treat cable-pairing permutations as a separate variable.
- [ ] **90 min:** Implement seeded generation of unique anchor and attachment sets, separation checks, and candidate pairing changes. Generate candidates from all families with a documented evaluation budget.
- [ ] **60 min:** Apply cheap reference-pose routing and Jacobian rank checks; keep failure counts by reason.
- [ ] **45 min:** Preserve A/B as reference baselines but do not force search to start from B.
- [ ] **30 min:** Save representative geometry figures and the random seed.

**Theory:** `C = {(A_i,r_i)}_(i=1..6)` and associated segment geometry `C_i(t)=B_i+t(A_i-B_i)`, `t in [0,1]`. **Evidence:** [R1] for reconfigurable attachments and workspace-constrained constraints.

**Output:** `B5_1_configuration_families.m`, `B5_1_candidate_pool.mat`, `B5_1_generation_notes.md`.

**Acceptance:** At least three different anchor-distribution families represented; pairing is stored explicitly; no global-optimality claim.

### Day 4 — Mon 9 Nov | B5.2 Multi-Pose Geometric Screening | 4–5 h

**Research question:** Can the new layouts avoid balloon penetration and cable interference on a useful initial test region?

- [ ] **45 min:** Reuse B3 segment–sphere and segment–segment functions; rerun known A/B cases as regression tests.
- [ ] **105 min:** Screen candidates using a **progressive sequence**: reference pose → declared core poses → fixed 189 target poses. Record failures by reason and orientation.
- [ ] **60 min:** Store minimum non-adjacent cable–cable distances and cable-to-surface signed margins. Treat zero-thickness contact tolerance as numerical, not hardware clearance.
- [ ] **40 min:** Discard severe collisions; retain several layouts for sensing assessment. Log computational budget.
- [ ] **20 min:** Summarise how many candidates survive each filter.

**Theory:** finite-line-segment minimum distance; nonpenetration and clearance constraints. **Evidence:** [R2] cable interference/reconfiguration and [R1] interference-free constraints.

**Output:** `B5_2_geometric_screening.m`, `B5_2_geometry_results.mat`, `B5_2_failure_summary.csv`.

**Acceptance:** Each failure has a reason; the exact pose set is identical for all candidate configurations; a mount/physical-cable clearance assumption remains explicitly unresolved if not specified.

### Day 5 — Tue 10 Nov | B5.3 Multi-Pose Sensing Quality and Ranking | 4–5 h

**Research question:** Among geometrically viable layouts, which have acceptable local length-to-pose sensitivity?

- [ ] **40 min:** Reuse and regression-test B4.1's analytical measurement Jacobian and scaled singular values.
- [ ] **90 min:** Evaluate rank, worst and median `sigma_min`, condition number, and assumed-noise covariance on feasible **core** and common **target** poses.
- [ ] **60 min:** Rank using **feasibility first**, then worst-case sensing quality; preserve trade-offs in a Pareto shortlist instead of combining uncalibrated weighted metrics.
- [ ] **50 min:** Compare A, B and new candidates using the same fixed pose set; report both coverage and uncertainty distributions.
- [ ] **25 min:** Export 5–10 promising candidates and a concise rationale.

**Theory:** `H_s = H diag(1,1,1,1/r_b,1/r_b,1/r_b)`; `sigma_min(H_s)`; `kappa(H_s)`; `Sigma_q ≈ (H' Sigma_L^(-1) H)^(-1)` when locally identifiable. **Evidence:** B4.1 results and Fisher-information / SVD theory; [R1] motivates workspace-based constraints. The 0.1 mm sensor noise is **assumed**.

**Output:** `B5_3_sensing_evaluation.m`, `B5_3_candidate_ranking.csv`, `B5_3_shortlist.mat`.

**Acceptance:** No candidate with severe geometric failure is reported as feasible; no arbitrary “Condition Number < 5” is claimed as a validated specification.

## 4. Week 2 — Provisional V1.0 and Flexible Compatibility (11–17 November)

### Day 6 — Wed 11 Nov | B5.4 Local Refinement and Validation | 4–5 h

**Research question:** Can a small number of geometric adjustments remove bottlenecks without sacrificing sensing quality?

- [ ] **60 min:** Diagnose worst-case cable pairs and attachment locations for 5–10 shortlisted candidates.
- [ ] **90 min:** Apply small, bounded changes to anchor coordinates, attachment spherical angles and/or pairing; enforce original mechanical region and separation constraints after each change.
- [ ] **50 min:** Re-evaluate all 189 training poses for shortlisted candidates; retain original A/B as references.
- [ ] **40 min:** Test extra poses not used for screening (intermediate translations and simultaneous-axis small rotations); record generalisation failures.
- [ ] **20 min:** Select 2–3 distinct finalists.

**Theory:** constrained local refinement; independent validation counters selection bias. **Evidence:** [R1], [R2]. Algorithm (bounded local search) is an **engineering implementation choice**.

**Output:** `B5_4_local_refinement.m`, `B5_4_validation_results.mat`, `B5_4_finalists.md`.

**Acceptance:** Finalists have reproducible 6×3 coordinate arrays and performance evidence on training and independent pose sets.

### Day 7 — Thu 12 Nov | B5.4 Prototype Configuration V1.0 Selection | 4–5 h

**Research question:** Is one six-cable geometry sufficiently safe and informative for initial, deliberately limited experiments?

- [ ] **45 min:** Compare finalists using geometry, clearance, Jacobian, uncertainty and mechanical accessibility; select **one provisional** V1.0 or document a blocker.
- [ ] **80 min:** Freeze six anchors, six rigid-body attachments and exact pairing; calculate `L_i(q)` at reference and target poses, plus sampled `L_min`, `L_max` and `Delta L` for each cable.
- [ ] **65 min:** Draft adjustable anchor rails, replaceable attachment fixtures, allowance for cable diameter, load-cell/encoder interfaces and optional future channels; do not dimension unvalidated mechanical features.
- [ ] **45 min:** Identify B6 sizing inputs still needed: rigid mass, payload/centre of mass, buoyancy/environment, tension control architecture, desired cable speed, minimum pretension, drum radius assumptions.

**Theory:** `L_i=||A_i-P-Rr_i||`, `Delta L_i=max_Q L_i-min_Q L_i`; attachment constraints [R1]. **Important:** sampled length range is not a guarantee over continuous or deformed motion.

**Output:** `B5_4_prototype_V1.mat`, `B5_4_prototype_V1_coordinates.csv`, `B5_4_cable_length_ranges.csv`, `B5_4_decision.md`.

### Day 8 — Fri 13 Nov | B5.5.1 Simplified Flexible Balloon Model | 4–5 h

**Research question:** How can a small, interpretable set of shape changes be introduced without delaying the prototype?

- [ ] **50 min:** Read [R3] for nonlinear spherical-membrane inflation assumptions and [R4] for model-constrained soft shape reconstruction; distinguish their physical models from the proposed simplification.
- [ ] **90 min:** Implement a **hypothetical affine ellipsoid**: `F(eta)=diag(1+eta_x,1+eta_y,1+eta_z)`; `B_i=P+R F(eta) r_i0`. Choose a small documented deformation sweep; do not label assumed deformation amplitudes clinically measured.
- [ ] **60 min:** Optionally impose `det F = 1` as an **approximately incompressible/volume-preserving scenario**, not a universal law for a fluid/gas balloon.
- [ ] **55 min:** Generate deformed surface meshes and attachment material-point positions; record assumptions and numerical validity limits.
- [ ] **20 min:** Save model file and scenario table.

**Theory:** affine map and deformation gradient `F`; ellipsoid semiaxes `a_j=r_b(1+eta_j)`; volume ratio `V/V0=det F`. For true elastomeric inflation, membrane stress and pressure need a nonlinear constitutive model [R3]. Shape priors/constraints are motivated by [R4].

**Output:** `B5_5_1_flexible_balloon_model.m`, `B5_5_1_deformation_cases.mat`, `B5_5_1_model_assumptions.md`.

**Acceptance:** Transparent distinction between the **engineering affine proxy** and a validated material model; surface and material points move consistently.

### Day 9 — Mon 16 Nov | B5.5.2–B5.5.3 Attachment Migration and Flexible Routing | 4–5 h

**Research question:** Can V1.0 anchors and attachment fixtures remain workable over the selected deformation cases?

- [ ] **60 min:** Compute attachment migration `Delta B_i=R(F-I)r_i0`; identify the largest material-point displacement and any attachment crowding.
- [ ] **90 min:** Recalculate cable vectors and lengths at deformed attachment points, compared with rigid baseline across the declared core test poses.
- [ ] **85 min:** Replace sphere-only collision logic with **line-segment vs deformed surface** checks and repeat cable–cable distances; check endpoint handling. Compare clearance and routing by case.
- [ ] **35 min:** Identify hardware implications: anchor rails, replaceable flexible attachments, local stress concentrations and additional cable travel requirements.
- [ ] **20 min:** Record which failures are geometric and which need a physical material test.

**Theory:** `B_i=P+R F r_i0`, `L_i=||A_i-B_i||`, `Delta B_i=R(F-I)r_i0`; collision constraints [R1], [R2]. Actual membrane stress/connections require [R3] and experiments.

**Output:** `B5_5_2_3_flexible_routing.m`, `B5_5_2_3_flexible_geometry_results.mat`, `B5_5_attachment_migration.png`.

**Acceptance:** At least rigid/no-deformation and multiple declared deformation cases compared under matching poses. A sphere-collision routine is **not** reused unchanged for an ellipsoid.

### Day 10 — Tue 17 Nov | B5.5.4–B5.5.5 Observability and B6 Handoff | 4–5 h

**Research question:** What do six lengths identify when rigid motion and deformation coexist, and can the V1.0 hardware be retained?

- [ ] **75 min:** Form an extended local measurement Jacobian `[H_rigid  H_def]` using finite differences for the selected deformation parameters; compute rank, singular values, null-space dimension and indicative rigid/deformation coupling.
- [ ] **55 min:** Explain that a 6×(6+m) Jacobian, `m>0`, cannot have full column rank without priors/constraints/auxiliary sensors. Do **not** treat rigid-configuration covariance as the flexible joint-state covariance.
- [ ] **60 min:** Decide: retain V1.0 anchor geometry, adjust attachment locations, or revisit full configuration. Record the required adjustable mount ranges and optional extra measurement channels.
- [ ] **50 min:** Create the B6 handoff: reference and sampled cable-length ranges (rigid and deformable), masses and external loads still needed, low-tension force-tracking assumptions, pulley/drum/encoder questions, unresolved tensile equilibrium.
- [ ] **25 min:** Archive two-week research findings and next-phase blockers; do not pre-plan a detailed next fortnight until this review.

**Theory:** `delta L ≈ H_rigid delta q + H_def delta eta`; `rank(H_total) <= 6` for six scalar length channels; local null space via SVD; [R4] supports model-based constraints/sensor augmentation. For later force-sizing, `W(q)T+w_ext=0` with `T_i >= 0` is a **separate mechanical feasibility model**, not a consequence of measurement-Jacobian rank.

**Output:** `B5_5_4_observability.m`, `B5_5_5_compatibility_decision.md`, `B6_sizing_input_checklist.md`, `two_week_review_2026_11_17.md`.

**Acceptance:** Honest V1.0 keep/adjust/reject decision; clear list of remaining measurements and B6 work. No unsupported claim that six cables independently recover six rigid DOFs **plus** general deformation.

**[R4] Liu, L., et al. (2025).** *Model-Based 3D Shape Reconstruction of Soft Robots via Distributed Strain Sensing.* Soft Robotics, 12(6), 721–731. DOI: **10.1089/soro.2024.0158**.

**Project-derived mathematical basis (not separate experimental paper):**

- Rigid cable geometry: `B_i=P+Rr_i`, `L_i=||A_i-B_i||`.
- Local measurement model: `delta L=H delta q + epsilon`; scaled Jacobian SVD and rank.
- Noise-limited local covariance: `Sigma_q≈(H' Sigma_L^(-1) H)^(-1)` under model assumptions.
- Affine deformation: `F=diag(lambda_x,lambda_y,lambda_z)`, `B_i=P+R F r_i0`; `det F` is the volume ratio under the affine map.
- Rigid/deformation identifiability: `H_total=[H_rigid,H_def]`, `rank(H_total) <= 6` with six cable measurements.
- Static tension (later B6): `W(q) T + w_external=0`, subject to cable-only positive tensions and actual low-disturbance tracking requirements.
